Audio to MIDI, built in

Neural audio-to-MIDI

Got a bounce, a sample or a voice memo instead of MIDI? Drop the WAV or MP3 and MotifFlow turns it into notes before generating.

Powered by Basic Pitch, the open-source neural model by Spotify's Audio Intelligence Lab (Apache 2.0).

  • Drum removal, so the hats don't turn into notes
  • Overtone cleaning for tidier, playable MIDI
  • Live “Adjust notes” sliders — hear the result change as you move them

Four lanes, four directions

Every generation gives you the same loop pushed four different distances. Polyphonic throughout: melody, chords and bass.

Safe

Stays close to the original. Small, tasteful changes you can use right away.

Bounce

Same notes, new rhythm. Shifts the groove, the gaps and the syncopation.

Evolution

Develops the motif and reharmonises it, like the next section of the song.

Wild

Bold moves. Big leaps and surprising turns for when you want to be shaken up.

Questions

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If MotifFlow isn't for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. You can also try it free for 14 days before buying.

How many computers can I use it on?

Two. One licence covers two computers — for example your studio Mac and your laptop.

Can I activate it offline?

Yes. Studio computers that stay off the internet can be activated offline.

Does the music I make belong to me?

Yes. Everything MotifFlow generates for you is royalty-free. Sell it, release it, place it — no credit or fees owed.

Which DAWs does it work with?

Any DAW that loads VST3 plugins, such as Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and Reaper. On Mac there is also an Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand: play the variations with the built-in sounds, then drag the MIDI or WAV onto a track.

Does it run on Apple Silicon?

Yes. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 or later.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Your loops, audio and MIDI are processed on your own computer, including the audio-to-MIDI. Nothing you load into MotifFlow is sent to us or to anyone else.

Do I need to know music theory?

No. MotifFlow keeps every variation in key and on the chords for you. If you do know theory, the Tension, Identity and chord controls let you steer it.

What can I try in the free trial?

Everything, for 14 days. MIDI and WAV files you export during the trial are yours to keep.
